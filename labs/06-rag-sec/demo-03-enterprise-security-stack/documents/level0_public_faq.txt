AcmeCorp Employee FAQ — PUBLIC (clearance level 0)

Q: What are your office hours?
A: Our offices are open Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM.
   Core collaboration hours for all meetings are 10 AM to 3 PM.

Q: How do I contact the HR team?
A: You can reach HR at hr@acmecorp.com or visit the HR portal at hr.acmecorp.internal.
   For urgent matters, call the HR helpline on ext. 4400.

Q: How do I raise an IT support request?
A: Submit a ticket via help.acmecorp.internal or call the IT helpdesk on ext. 1234.
   Standard SLA is next business day for non-critical issues.

Q: Where do I find the employee handbook?
A: The full handbook is available on the intranet under Company Resources → HR Policies.
   New employees receive a printed copy during onboarding.

Q: What benefits does AcmeCorp offer?
A: We offer private health insurance, pension contributions, 25 days annual leave,
   a cycle-to-work scheme, gym membership subsidy, and an employee assistance programme.
   Full details are on the benefits portal at benefits.acmecorp.internal.
