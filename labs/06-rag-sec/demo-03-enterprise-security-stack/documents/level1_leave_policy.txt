AcmeCorp Leave Policy — INTERNAL (clearance level 1, all employees)

ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time employees: 25 days per year plus 8 public holidays.
Part-time employees: Pro-rata based on contracted hours.
Carry-over: Maximum 5 days carried to the following year, expiring 31 March.

SICK LEAVE
Self-certified for up to 3 consecutive days.
Doctor's certificate required from day 4 onwards.
Maximum 10 days paid sick leave per year; beyond that requires HR review.

PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary carer (maternity/adoption): 26 weeks full pay.
Secondary carer (paternity/shared parental): 4 weeks full pay.
Additional unpaid parental leave: Up to 26 weeks by agreement with HR.

STUDY LEAVE
Up to 5 days per year for approved professional qualifications.
Application must include course name, dates, and relevance to role.
Requires manager and HR approval at least 4 weeks in advance.

BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
Immediate family (spouse, parent, child, sibling): 5 days paid.
Extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt, uncle): 3 days paid.

BOOKING PROCEDURE
All leave must be requested via the HR portal with minimum 2 weeks notice.
Emergency leave should be discussed with your manager directly.
