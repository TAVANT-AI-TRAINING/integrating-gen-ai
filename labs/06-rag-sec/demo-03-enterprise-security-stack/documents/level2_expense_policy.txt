AcmeCorp Expense Policy — RESTRICTED (clearance level 2, managers and above)

HOME OFFICE EQUIPMENT
Up to £500 per calendar year for remote workers.
Eligible items: desk, ergonomic chair, monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset.
All purchases require receipts and must be pre-approved by your manager.

TRAVEL
Economy flights only (booked through the travel portal).
Standard rail (not first class). Personal mileage: £0.45 per mile.
Taxis/ride-share permitted when public transport is impractical.

MEALS AND ENTERTAINMENT
Daily meal allowance during overnight travel: £30.
Client entertainment: max £75 per person — requires Director pre-approval.
Team meals (5+ people): max £40 per person — requires VP pre-approval.

MOBILE PHONE
£25 per month allowance for employees regularly using personal phones for business.
Company devices are available from IT for roles with high mobile requirements.

DEPARTMENT BUDGETS
Managers may approve expense claims up to £1,000 without escalation.
Claims between £1,000 and £5,000 require Director approval.
Claims above £5,000 require VP approval and a written business case.

SUBMISSION
Submit all claims within 30 days via the Expenses portal.
Receipts required for all amounts over £10.
Late submissions (>30 days) may be declined unless exceptional circumstances apply.
