AcmeCorp Compensation Bands — CONFIDENTIAL (clearance level 3, HR and executives only)

BASE SALARY RANGES (2025)

Grade 1 — Junior Associate:       £28,000 – £35,000
Grade 2 — Associate:              £35,000 – £50,000
Grade 3 — Senior Associate:       £50,000 – £75,000
Grade 4 — Manager / Tech Lead:    £75,000 – £110,000
Grade 5 — Senior Manager / Director: £110,000 – £160,000
Grade 6 — VP / Head of Function:  £160,000 – £220,000
Grade 7 — C-Suite / EVP:          £220,000 – £450,000

BONUS TARGETS (% of base salary)
Grade 1–2: 5% – 10%
Grade 3–4: 10% – 20%
Grade 5–6: 20% – 35%
Grade 7:   35% – 60% plus equity vesting

EQUITY PROGRAMME
Grades 5–7 are eligible for RSU grants reviewed annually.
C-Suite packages include performance-linked options reviewed by the Remuneration Committee.
