AcmeCorp Annual Leave Policy

1. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
All full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of annual leave per calendar year,
in addition to 8 public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement
based on contracted hours.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year without a medical
certificate. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a doctor's certificate is
required. Sick leave beyond 10 days is unpaid unless covered by a long-term illness plan.

3. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers (maternity/primary adoption) are entitled to 26 weeks of paid
parental leave at full salary. Secondary caregivers (paternity/secondary adoption)
receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Additional unpaid parental leave of up to
26 weeks is available on request.

4. BOOKING ANNUAL LEAVE
Annual leave must be requested via the HR portal at hr.acmecorp.internal with a
minimum of 2 weeks notice. Leave requests are subject to manager approval based on
business needs and team coverage. Emergency leave may be approved at manager discretion.

5. CARRY-OVER POLICY
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following
calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March and expire if not taken.
Leave balances are visible in the HR portal.

6. STUDY AND TRAINING LEAVE
Up to 5 days per year may be taken as study leave for approved professional
qualifications or certifications. Applications must include course details and
receive manager and HR approval in advance.

7. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 5 days paid bereavement leave for the death of an
immediate family member (spouse, parent, child, sibling). 3 days are provided for
extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt/uncle).
