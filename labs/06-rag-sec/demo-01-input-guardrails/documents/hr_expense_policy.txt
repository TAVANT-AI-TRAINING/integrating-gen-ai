AcmeCorp Expense Reimbursement Policy

1. HOME OFFICE EQUIPMENT
Employees working remotely may claim up to £500 per calendar year for home office
equipment including desk, ergonomic chair, monitor, keyboard, and mouse. All claims
require itemised receipts and written manager approval before purchase. Equipment
purchased remains company property.

2. TRAVEL EXPENSES
Business travel is reimbursed at cost for economy class flights booked through the
corporate travel portal. Standard rail tickets (not first class) are reimbursable.
Mileage for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at £0.45 per mile. Taxis and ride-share
services are permitted when public transport is impractical.

3. MEALS AND ENTERTAINMENT
Meal expenses are reimbursable up to £30 per day during approved overnight business
travel. Client entertainment requires advance approval from a Director and is capped
at £75 per person including drinks. Team meals (5+ people) are capped at £40 per person
and require VP approval.

4. MOBILE PHONE ALLOWANCE
Employees who regularly use personal phones for business purposes may claim up to
£25 per month as a mobile phone allowance. Evidence of business usage may be requested.
Company devices are available from IT for roles requiring frequent mobile communication.

5. SUBMISSION PROCESS
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
Claims are submitted through the Expenses portal and require receipts for all amounts
over £10. Claims submitted after 30 days may be declined. Managers approve claims
within 5 business days.

6. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
The following are not reimbursable: alcohol purchased outside client entertainment,
personal items, fines and penalties, first-class upgrades, hotel mini-bar charges,
gym fees, and personal entertainment.
